## Measurement plan

- Organic impressions and clicks by page and query.
- Ranking movement for service-plus-city searches.
- Click-to-call events.
- Estimate-form starts and completions.
- Lead source, project type, city, budget range, and lead quality.
- Core Web Vitals and mobile usability.
- Indexed pages, crawl issues, rich-result errors, and page-two opportunities.

## Guardrails

- Improve existing pages before creating overlapping pages.
- Do not create thin city pages, duplicate service pages, or doorway pages.
- Use verified business details and authentic project evidence.
- Keep each page focused on a real user need and a clear construction service.
- Deploy only after build, accessibility, form, navigation, and responsive checks pass.
